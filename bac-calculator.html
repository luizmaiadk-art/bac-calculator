This is a rough statistical estimate. Real BAC depends on food, hydration, sleep, medication, and individual metabolism — variation between people can easily be ±30%. The plausible range above reflects this uncertainty; never rely on the lower bound.

Measurable impairment in reaction time and divided attention begins around 0.2‰ — well below the 0.5‰ legal limit. Driving while visibly impaired is illegal under færdselsloven § 54 regardless of BAC.

Denmark's legal limit is 0.5‰ (standard licence). The legally valid measurements are an evidential breath test (alkomat) or blood test — roadside screening is preliminary only. If there is any doubt, do not drive.
— EST. SAFETY MARGIN ADVISED —
Model accuracy is further reduced by: age (older adults have lower body water, higher BAC) · dehydration (raises effective BAC) · carbonated drinks (faster absorption) · certain medications (metformin, antihistamines, antidepressants). When any of these apply, treat the upper bound of the range as your reference.
